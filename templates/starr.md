# STARR (Situation-Task-Action-Result-Reflection)

## Situation
[What was the context?]

## Task
[What were you responsible for?]

## Action
[What did you specifically do?]

## Result
[What happened because of it? Be specific — numbers, outcomes, who noticed.]

## Reflection
[What would you do differently, or what did this teach you?]
